# Proposed Experiment 08H: active identification under unknown change time

**DRAFT ONLY — NOT PREREGISTERED OR RUN.** We will not use final held-out seeds before registering the protocol.

The 08G simulator intentionally reveals that an attribution window has begun, and its programmed observer enumerates the exact generative families. The next development phase should remove these shortcuts:

1. World without a change-point cue, plus a genuine unchanged/null condition.
2. Hidden combinations of actuator change, exogenous motion, visual drift, and stochastic one-step impulses; some may be observationally equivalent.
3. Policy must select when to issue informative movements versus pursue external goals, with real costs for zero-action probing and sensor consultation.
4. Train at least one hypothesis-discovery method on past action–observation data without hidden cause labels; compare to Bayes filters and system-ID baselines allowed the same sensor information.
5. Evaluate predictive model quality *and* causal attribution under interventions; report explicit unidentifiable cases as ambiguous rather than erroneous forced classifications.
6. Structural mutation/evolution can be an additional arm, not the default definition of success. Compare equal optimization budgets, failure rates, and generalized interventions.
7. Perform method reviews, development tuning and test-power planning first. Freeze all code, data generation, policies, hyperparameters, primary comparisons and exclusions; record them with an independent timestamp before using untouched confirmatory seeds.

A high classification score alone, especially with model families hardwired into the observer, would not constitute a new finding about consciousness.
