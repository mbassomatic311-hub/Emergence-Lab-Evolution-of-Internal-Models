Can a system move beyond its senses?

What if your senses hide the truth?

All agents begin with three crude symbols. Different physical conditions can look identical. Genetic mutations may create a new, costly measurement ability—and evolution decides whether that ability survives.

Unlike earlier experiments, the agents must pay to gather extra evidence. We ask whether natural selection favors discovering hidden differences, and whether that ability survives misleading information or environmental change.

What did the simulation actually give them?

We must distinguish genuinely selected abilities from features already supplied by the model.

Supplied by us

A three-symbol sensor, an environment with latent safe/danger states, a mutation mechanism, costly optional measurement hardware, and seven evolvable behavioral parameters.

Not individually programmed

Which lineages acquire the hardware, when they choose to measure, how they use the readings, and which policies become common after many generations.

What the experiment tests

Whether adaptive selection can favor evidence gathering that resolves ambiguities left by a coarse sensory interface.

What it cannot show

That agents know their limitations, have subjective feelings, or can access an observer-independent reality. Behavioral success is not proof of consciousness.

All conditions use the changing environment unless labeled stable. Net payoff includes energy costs. These outcomes depend on our chosen rules, costs, and mutation rates; this is an illustrative evolutionary-computation experiment, not empirical evidence about prehistoric brains.

In the truthful-measurement condition, the population can evolve an ability to gather information that its original sensor throws away. In controls where the information is false, unavailable, or prohibitively expensive, that advantage disappears.

Method: artificial agents, selection proportional to simulated net payoff, mutable genetic decision rules, and a stochastic extra sensor. All generations, the hidden environment, and the measurement mechanism are engineered. No consciousness criterion or experience detector is included.
