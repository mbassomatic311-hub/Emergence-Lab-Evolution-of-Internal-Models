# Research status and interpretation

The numerical findings are generated by artificial simulations, not organisms. The task structure, sensors, mutation operators, genotypes, costs and selection rules were specified by the developer. Consequently, the observed evolution of useful behavior is neither unprogrammed natural life nor evidence of phenomenal consciousness.

**Experiments 01–05**: educational/exploratory evolutionary-computation demonstrations. Many possible mechanisms were encoded in the action space or genotype. Earlier plots and summaries should not be described as confirmatory findings.

**Experiment 06**: locally frozen computational comparison, with post-hoc de-novo no-inheritance follow-up noted separately; consult `experiments/exp06/PUBLICATION_AUDIT_ADDENDUM.md`. The no-inheritance baseline in the original design had a confound, later investigated; never silently combine those analyses.

**Experiment 07**: locally frozen 32-seed primary comparison after pilot work, not publicly preregistered. Memory and action-conditioned sensory features were supplied to the architecture and were not independently invented. Interpret performance on actuator reversal as task-specific sensorimotor adaptation.

**Recommended language:** "In deliberately constructed simulation environments, evolutionary operators favored information-processing structures useful for task-specific decision-making." Avoid "we created consciousness", "we discovered the origin of awareness", or "we proved reality is an interface".

Before academic submission: replicate the study with independent implementation, publish a held-out design registered before data collection, add ablations, report compute/hardware versions, conduct literature and novelty review, and seek external methodological feedback.
